FAQ

Common questions

Does TinyHand Windows require installation?

No. It runs as a self-contained portable executable (TinyHandWindows.exe). Place it on your desktop, in Downloads, or on a USB drive without administrator permissions.

Does it work with Zoom, Teams, and OBS?

Yes. The hand pointer and annotations draw on a layered transparent Win32 desktop window, so screen-sharing and recording apps capture everything cleanly.

Can I upload custom hands and sounds?

Yes. Open Settings to import any transparent PNG as a custom hand pointer or upload custom WAV and MP3 tap sound files.

How does this relate to macOS TinyHand?

It shares the spirit of Kamran Ahmed’s macOS app (nilbuild/tinyhand), rebuilt natively for Windows with custom studio settings and MIT licensing.
