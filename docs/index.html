Frequently Asked Questions

Does Wajji TinyHand PRO require installation?

No! It runs as a self-contained single executable (WajjiTinyHandPRO.exe). You can place it on your Desktop, USB flash drive, or run it directly without any administrator permissions.

Does it work with Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet?

Yes! The pointer and annotations render on a hardware-accelerated Win32 layered transparent desktop window. Any screen sharing software captures your hand and annotations in real time.

Can I upload my own custom hands and sounds?

Yes. The settings studio includes an integrated Custom Hand Uploader (transparent PNG) and an Audio Uploader for any WAV or MP3 tap sound.

How is this inspired by macOS TinyHand?

We love the design philosophy of Kamran Ahmed's macOS app (nilbuild/tinyhand). We rebuilt and optimized the engine natively for Windows with full attribution under the MIT license.
